# Project Methodology

## 1. Data Understanding
The dataset contains 588,101 user records, including experiment group, conversion status, advertising exposure frequency, and the day and hour associated with maximum ad exposure.

## 2. Data Quality Validation
Validated record count, unique user IDs, duplicates, missing values, conversion values and advertising exposure values. No invalid records were identified in the completed checks.

## 3. Descriptive Analysis
Compared treatment and control conversion rates and investigated conversion patterns across:
- Six advertising exposure ranges
- Seven days of the week
- Twenty-four hours of the day

## 4. Statistical Analysis
Applied a two-tailed two-proportion z-test at a 5% significance level to evaluate the difference between treatment and control conversion rates.

Calculated the absolute conversion-rate difference, relative lift, p-value and 95% confidence interval.

## 5. Business Intelligence Reporting
Developed a Power BI report with four pages:
- Campaign Overview
- Ad Exposure Analysis
- Day & Hour Analysis
- Statistical Findings

## 6. Interpretation
Findings were interpreted descriptively. The relationship between advertising exposure and conversion was not treated as proof of causality. The unequal treatment and control group sizes were considered when interpreting results.
